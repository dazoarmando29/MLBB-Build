🛡️ Universal Tank Build (Roam / EXP — Patch 2.2.16 meta)

🌱 New? Boots first. Then buy the armor that matches what kills you — sword/physical damage → Antique Cuirass, purple magic damage → Athena's Shield. Immortality last. That's 90% of tanking.

Rule from pro guides: stack HP first, then counter the main enemy damage. Tough Boots (vs CC/magic) or Warrior Boots (vs physical). Antique Cuirass vs skill/physical burst, Athena's Shield vs magic burst, Radiant Armor vs magic DPS, Dominance Ice vs heal + attack speed, Blade Armor vs crit MM, Immortality last. Click any tank below for its personal best build.

  1. 1. Tough Boots / Warrior Boots — pick by enemy main damage. Tough Boots in 61% of tank builds (30% CC reduction).
  2. 2. Antique Cuirass CORE — +920 HP, reduces physical skill damage. Buy first vs MM/Fighter.
  3. 3. Athena's Shield CORE — magic shield, mandatory vs burst mage (Eudora, Aurora, Kadita).
  4. 4. Dominance Ice SITUATIONAL — anti-heal aura + hybrid defense vs Estes, Uranus, Esmeralda.
  5. 5. Radiant Armor / Blade Armor — Radiant vs sustained magic (Chang'e, Yve, Zhuxin), Blade vs crit (Lesley, Bruno, Melissa).
  6. 6. Immortality LATE — in 85% of tank builds, always 5th–6th slot. Turns engages into winning trades.
🛡️ Emblem: Tank — Vitality + Tenacity + Concussive Blast  |  Spell: Flicker / Revitalize  |  Order: Boots → counter-item-1 → opposite-resist → Immortality → situational.

🔥 Damage Builds — Assassin / Fighter / Burst Mage

🌱 New? Finish ONE damage item fully before starting the next — half-built items do nothing. If you play assassin, you must get kills early or you'll feel useless late. That's normal.

Physical Assassin (Jungle): Rapid Boots → Blade of Heptaseas → Hunter Strike → Blade of Despair → Malefic Roar → Immortality. Rush first core before 4 min to snowball. If behind after 8 min, swap BOD for Malefic Roar + Immortality.
Fighter (EXP): Tough Boots → War Axe (63% of fighter builds, avg slot 2.3 — stacks true damage) → Hunter Strike / Endless Battle → Blade of Despair → Malefic Roar (vs 2+ armor) → Immortality / Queen's Wings.
Burst Mage: Arcane Boots → Glowing Wand / Clock of Destiny → Holy Crystal → Divine Glaive (96% of mage builds — mandatory vs Athena) → Blood Wings → Winter Crown.

Emblem: Assassin — Rupture + Master Assassin + Killing Spree (physical) / Mage — Inspire + Master Assassin + Lethal Ignition (burst) / Fighter — Thrill + Weapon Master + War Cry. Spells: Retribution (jungle), Flicker / Execute (lane).

💚 Support Builds — Heal, Peel & Vision

🌱 New? Your job isn't kills. Buy Fleeting Time + Oracle, stand BEHIND your damage dealers, and press heal/ult when a fight starts. Dying first = lost fight.

Supports buy roam boots first, then counter items, never full damage. Core: Favor / Encourage roam + Tough Boots → Dominance Ice (vs heal) or Athena's Shield (vs burst) → Oracle (boosts your own heal/shield) → Radiant Armor → Immortality → Guardian Helmet. Anti-heal is on YOU if nobody else buys it. 79% of support builds finish with Immortality.

Emblem: Support — Agility + Recovery / Bargain Hunter + Pull Yourself Together / Focusing Mark  |  Spell: Flicker / Revitalize / Purify. Always keep a slot flexible for Glowing Wand (mage support) vs heavy regen.

🏹 Marksman Builds — Crit vs Attack-Speed

🌱 New? Stay far away, keep hitting, never stand still. Buy Windtalker first almost always. If an assassin jumps you, that's what Wind of Nature is for — press it, then run to your tank.

Crit route (Melissa, Beatrix, Bruno, Lesley, Clint): Swift Boots → Windtalker → Berserker's Fury → Scarlet Phantom → Malefic Roar (mandatory vs 2 armor items) → Blade of Despair / Immortality.
Attack-speed / shred route (Karrie, Claude, Moskov, Hanabi, Kimmy): Swift Boots → Windtalker → Golden Staff / Corrosion Scythe → Demon Hunter Sword ( shreds high-HP tanks — 8% current HP per hit) → Wind of Nature (vs assassin dive, buy 3rd if Hayabusa/Ling) → Immortality.
Vs heal: Sea Halberd 4th slot. Never buy BOD while behind — buy 2 cheap items instead.

Emblem: Marksman — Fatal + Weapon Master + Weakness Finder (kite) or Quantum Charge (vs dive). Spell: Flicker / Inspire / Purify.

🧭 Lane Guide — what is my job?

🌱 New? Pick ONE lane and learn it first. Your job is: win your lane quietly, then help at Turtle. Dying less matters more than killing more.

⚔️ EXP Lane (top) — the 1v1 island

🌱 Your job: survive and out-level the enemy fighter. You are on your own — nobody will help you early.
First 3 minutes: last-hit minions, hit level 2 and 4 BEFORE them (that's when you can fight). Don't chase under their tower.
Your big moment: walk down to the Turtle fight when it spawns — a level ahead wins it. Then go back to your lane.
Win condition: you become the teamfight frontline. Build 2 damage items, then full defense.
⛔ Don't: push to their tower with no vision — that's where junglers wait to kill you.
Easy starters: Balmond, Zilong, Alucard, Belerick · Spells: Flicker / Vengeance · Find EXP heroes →

🔪 Core / Jungle — the hunter

🌱 Your job: farm monsters fast, hit level 4 first, then gank (surprise-attack) a lane. You decide who wins early.
First 3 minutes: take blue buff → red buff → little monsters, in that flow. Take Retribution spell — it's required. Hit level 4, then visit the weakest enemy lane.
Your big moment: Turtle and Lord. You + Retribution secure them. Never start one when the enemy jungler is alive and near.
Win condition: get fed (ahead in gold), delete their damage dealers before fights start. One full damage item before 4 minutes = snowball.
⛔ Don't: keep farming while your team fights 4v5 next to you. Come first, farm later.
Easy starters: Saber, Balmond, Alucard, Martis (skip Fanny/Ling/Hayabusa until later — 🔥 Tricky) · Spell: Retribution, always

🏹 Gold Lane (bottom) — the late-game cannon

🌱 Your job: farm safely and DON'T die. You are the weakest early and the strongest late — every death delays that.
First 3 minutes: last-hit every minion, stay near your tower. The gold-siege minion is worth extra — don't miss it.
Your big moment: after 2 full items you shred everything. Push your tower only right after winning a fight, with teammates.
Win condition: stay alive in teamfights behind your tank and hit nonstop. Positioning beats damage.
⛔ Don't: trade hits early with fighters, or push alone with no vision — assassins live for that.
Easy starters: Layla, Miya, Clint, Hanabi · Spells: Flicker / Inspire · Buy Wind of Nature 3rd if assassins dive you

🔮 Mid Lane (bonus) — the helper

🌱 Your job: clear your wave FAST, then walk to help gold lane or turtle. Mid players who never leave mid lose games.
First 3 minutes: clear wave → check turtle/gold lane → go back. Repeat. Buy Demon Shoes if you run out of mana.
⛔ Don't: stand still in the open — mages get assassinated first. Move with your roamer.
Easy starters: Eudora, Nana, Gord, Cyclops, Aurora · Spell: Flicker

💚 Roam — the bodyguard (your Minotaur lives here!)

🌱 Your job: protect your damage dealers, start good fights, and give vision (stand in bushes so enemies can't hide). You don't take minions — your gold comes from the roam boots.
First 3 minutes: buy roam boots FIRST, help mid clear, then guard your jungler at buffs or babysit gold lane. Check bushes before your carry walks in.
Your big moment: every Turtle/Lord — arrive FIRST, hide in the bush, start the fight when your team is ready.
Win condition: your carry survives because of you. Engage when grouped, peel (push divers off) when they dive.
⛔ Don't: take your carry's minions, face-check bushes alone late game, or ult 1 enemy while their team kills your 4 teammates.
Easy starters: Minotaur, Tigreal, Franco, Rafaela · Spells: Flicker / Revitalize · As Minotaur: fight when rage is FULL, wait when it's not
